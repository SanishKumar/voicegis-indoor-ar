# Asterion University Medical Center

Asterion is an original, fictional four-level academic medical center used to
exercise VoiceGIS at a more credible demo scale.

It is not a surveyed venue, a reconstruction of a real hospital, or safe for
physical navigation. Names and geometry are invented. Public hospital
wayfinding material informed the broad design patterns only: a central
concourse, landmark atrium, multiple vertical-circulation options, public and
restricted clinical zones, accessible routes, and visitor services.

## Benchmark scope

- 4 levels and a 72 m × 32 m primary footprint
- 60 semantic spaces
- Two accessible lift banks and two public stair systems
- Public specialty clinics, diagnostics, family services, education, and food
- Explicit restricted clinical and operations spaces
- Reproducible QR/AprilTag anchor locations

The small `reference-medical-centre` fixture remains the deterministic
regression package. Asterion is the product demonstration benchmark.
